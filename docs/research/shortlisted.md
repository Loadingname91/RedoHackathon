# Idea list: Crimson Hacks 2026 (State of Utah)

1. **Interest radar for meeting people** (Ideas.md, U-Pulse, CommonGround, commuter micro-connection, PocketHang)
   Check into a campus spot or event for a set window. Get matched with people who share your interests and are open to talking right now. Meet at a suggested spot once both of you accept.

2. **Accessible campus router** (AccessU, AccessPlan, accessible path + social overlay)
   Campus directions that account for slope, snow, ice, broken elevators and door buttons, tailored to a mobility profile like wheelchair or crutches. Can also check whether you can reach a specific event.

3. **Campus barrier reporter + accommodation helper** (U-Access Vision)
   Snap a photo of a broken elevator, blocked ramp or broken door button. AI labels it, pins it on a campus map and drafts a repair request. Also guides students through getting disability accommodations.

4. **Free-time event matcher**
   Import your class schedule and get campus events that fit the gaps between classes, including walking time.

5. **Open table for spontaneous meals**
   Say "I'm eating alone at the Union right now, join me" so others nearby can drop in. Spontaneous, not scheduled in advance.

6. **Event buddy: arrive or leave together** (WalkIn Together, LeaveTogether)
   First-time attendees are grouped into twos or threes with a meeting point and a host who welcomes them. People leaving toward the same stop can also find each other.

7. **Sensory-friendly spaces finder** (SensoryMap/FocusPod, QuietU)
   Live campus map of how loud and crowded study and social spaces are, using phone mic readings and reports that expire. Optional silent study-buddy matching.

8. **Structured event access info** (AccessCard)
   Paste an event listing. AI pulls out the access details (entrance, seating, captions, quiet space) and flags what's missing. The organizer confirms, and the app publishes a clear access card.

9. **Group conversation help for Deaf/HoH students** (CaptionCircle)
   A shared screen for club conversations: current topic, typed contributions, a discreet "please repeat" button and a queue for taking turns.

10. **Accessible trails + adaptive outdoor buddies**
    Find Utah trails that fit your ability (grade, surface, air quality, weather) and match with others who have similar adaptive needs.

11. **Ski-day canyon carpool optimizer** (carpool matcher, CanyonPool 4+)
    Match skiers into carpools by seats, gear space, snow tires and departure time, so cars reach the 3+ or 4+ riders needed for cheap or free parking at Brighton and Solitude.

12. **Best time to leave for the Cottonwood Canyons**
    Suggest departure times that avoid the ski-day traffic jam, updating with weather and demand.

13. **Inversion / air-quality planner** (activity recommender, exposure planner, inversion social pivot)
    Enter an activity and how sensitive you are to bad air. Using live air-quality data, get a better time, a higher location or an indoor alternative, including indoor social options on bad-air days.

14. **Great Salt Lake dust visualizer**
    Move a slider to change the lake level and see how much lakebed is exposed and how far dust could spread.

15. **Local Utah makers & shops discovery**
    Find nearby makers, markets and shops by interest, with optional event or collaboration matching.
